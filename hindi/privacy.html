Bolo! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Bolo! is made by TutorWise (tutorwise.ca).

In short: Bolo keeps only what it needs to save your learning progress. There are no ads, and we never sell or share your information.

What Bolo stores

  • Your learning progress — XP, finished lessons, scores, streak, collected cards and your settings (including the name you choose). This is saved on your device.
  • Your account, if you create one — your email address and a password. Your progress is then also saved online so it isn't lost if you change phones.
  • Simple usage numbers — how many times a day you open Bolo, roughly how many minutes you use it, and minutes of hands-free practice. These are saved with your progress and are used only to improve Bolo.

Where it is kept

Accounts and online progress are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Only your own signed-in account can read your saved progress.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the microphone or use hands-free practice with “Listen to me”, Bolo uses your iPhone's built-in speech recognition to check your Hindi. Apple may process that audio to turn it into text, under Apple's privacy policy. Bolo does not record, keep or upload your voice — it only compares the text with the right answer. Hindi and English voices are your phone's own voices.

Movie clips

Film clips in the Filmi Album are official videos played from YouTube, which is run by Google and has its own privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. No message is sent through our servers.

Deleting your information

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your login and your progress saved online.
  • Progress on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Bolo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through tutorwise.ca/contact.
