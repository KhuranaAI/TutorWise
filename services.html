Our Services

In-person K–12 tutoring in Brampton. Tailored academic support across the full range of Ontario curriculum subjects — built around where each student actually is.

Personalized Support, K–12

Every student at TutorWise receives targeted support on their own material and goals. We don't apply a standardized program across the group. We find out what each student needs, build a plan around that, and adapt it as they progress.

Before sessions begin, every student goes through an in-person assessment so we have a clear starting point. That shapes everything that follows — the subjects we focus on, the pace, and the goals.

Who We Work With

We support students across all K–12 grade levels, with instruction matched to Ontario curriculum expectations at each stage.

  • Kindergarten – Grade 2  (Early Learners) Early literacy, phonics, reading foundations, number sense, and building positive learning habits from the start.
  • Grades 3 – 5  (Upper Elementary) Reading comprehension, writing structure, multiplication and division, fractions, geometry, and EQAO readiness at Grade 3.
  • Grades 6 – 8  (Middle School) A critical transition period where academic expectations rise sharply. Math, English, and science — with a focus on developing the study habits and independent work skills students will need in high school.
  • Grades 9 – 10  (Early High School) Support through the fully destreamed Grade 9 curriculum and the more differentiated Grade 10 year. Credit support, academic confidence, and preparation for senior-level coursework.
  • Grades 11 – 12  (Senior High School) Advanced support across university, college, mixed, and open pathway courses. Higher-order thinking, OSSLT prep, and post-secondary readiness in math, science, and English.

Subjects We Cover

We teach the full range of Ontario curriculum subjects, with specialized support in the areas where students need it most.

📐

Mathematics

Kindergarten through Grade 12. Number sense, fractions, algebra, geometry, functions, data management, and calculus. We address root misunderstandings, not just surface-level mistakes before a test.

📖

English and Literacy

Reading comprehension, paragraph and essay writing, grammar, vocabulary, and literary analysis. Support for all grade levels, including reluctant readers and writers as well as students strengthening their academic writing.

🔬

Science

Biology, chemistry, and physics for Grades 6–12. We make abstract concepts concrete, reinforce understanding before the exam, and help students prepare for science-heavy high school courses.

🎯

IEP Support

We work alongside your child's Individual Education Plan. Our tutors adapt instruction to match identified learning needs and keep parents informed on what is working and what may need adjusting.

🌍

ESL Support

English language development for students at all proficiency levels. Vocabulary, reading, writing, grammar, and spoken English connected to what students are expected to do in school.

📝

Exam and Test Preparation

EQAO, OSSLT, and subject-specific high school exams. We focus on question familiarity, time management, test strategy, and building the confidence to perform under pressure.

📚

Study Skills and Homework Help

For students who struggle to work independently or stay organized. We teach the study strategies and habits that make a real difference over a full semester.

🎓

Academic Pathway Support

Targeted support for students in Grades 10–12 navigating university, college, and mixed-level courses. Also available for IB program students and SHSM coursework.
