What Happens After You Reach Out

A clear, three-step process from your first call to your child's first session.

1

We Talk

A free 15-minute call to understand your child's situation, what's not working, and what you're hoping to change. No forms, no paperwork. Just an honest conversation about where your child is and what they need.

2

In-Person Assessment

Your child comes in for an in-person assessment so we can identify their current level and where the gaps are. This gives us a real starting point rather than guessing, and shapes everything that follows.

3

Sessions Begin

We build a personalized learning plan and your child begins attending small-group sessions with individualized support. We keep you updated along the way so you always know what's improving and what we're working on.
