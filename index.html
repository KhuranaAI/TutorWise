Structured Support for Students Who Need More

TutorWise is an in-person learning centre in Brampton supporting K–12 students who are falling behind, preparing for important tests, building academic confidence, or navigating IEP and ESL needs.

We find out where a student is struggling, build a plan that reflects their actual starting point, and help them make real progress. Every student gets focused attention on their specific subject material and goals.

What We Offer

In-person sessions across the full range of K–12 subjects. Each student receives focused support on their specific material and goals.

📐

Mathematics (K–12)

From number sense in early grades to functions, calculus, and data management in high school. We address the root misunderstandings, not just the symptoms on the test.

📖

English and Literacy

Reading comprehension, writing, grammar, and vocabulary development across all grade levels. Support for reluctant readers and writers as well as students aiming to strengthen their academic writing.

🔬

Science

Science support across middle school and high school, including biology, chemistry, and physics. Concept-first teaching that makes the material make sense before the exam.

🎯

IEP Support

We work alongside your child's existing school plan. Our tutors adapt instruction to match identified learning needs and keep parents informed on what is working and where adjustments may be needed.

🌍

ESL Support

Language development support for English language learners at any grade level. Vocabulary, reading, writing, grammar, and spoken English connected to what students are expected to do in school.

📝

Exam Preparation

Targeted prep for EQAO, OSSLT, and subject-specific high school exams. We focus on question types, time management, and building the confidence to sit a test without blanking out.

📚

Study Skills and Homework Help

For students who struggle to work independently or manage their workload. We teach the study strategies and organizational habits that make a real difference over a full semester.

🔄

Academic Pathway Support

Support for students navigating applied to academic transitions, IB programs, SHSM coursework, and senior high school university preparation in Grades 11 and 12.

How It Works

Getting started is a clear, three-step process. See the full process here.

1

You Reach Out

A quick call or message to understand your child's situation, grade level, and what they need support with.

2

In-Person Assessment

Your child comes in for an in-person assessment so we can identify their current level and where the gaps are — giving us an accurate starting point.

3

Sessions Begin

We build a personalized learning plan and your child begins sessions. The plan is refined over the first few weeks as we get to know how your child works.
